## Question3
```
Interesting. Travel budgets can impact the activities people choose on a trip, such as deciding between eating at local restaurants or preparing their own meals. Do you think setting a strict budget helps people enjoy their vacations more or less?
Why or why not?
```

### Brush up
```
I think it's better not to set a strict budget for a trip.
Because if I decide the budget in advance, it makes me less willing to try new things or spend money on unexpected experiences.
One of the main purpose for traveling is to have new experiences and feel strong emotions,
so I want to leave some room to do whatever I want as much as possible.
```
