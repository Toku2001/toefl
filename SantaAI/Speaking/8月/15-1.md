```

```
I think working in an office will decrease, 
but it won’t disappear completely. 
Compared to working remotely, having face-to-face conversations helps improve teamwork 
because it’s harder to sense other people’s emotions when working remotely. 
In my opinion, working in an office will remain common for the next decade.
