## Subject
```
Researchers are interested in understanding people's experiences with traveling to new places. 
You will have a short online interview with a researcher. The researcher will ask you some questions.
```

### Question1
```
Thanks for joining the study. Today, I'd like to ask you some questions about traveling to new places. 
First, can you tell me about a memorable trip you took to a place you had never visited before?
```

#### Ans1
```
My most memorable trip was to go to Australia 
because it was the first time I had ever traveled abroad. 
I really enjoyed the unique atmosphere, including the architecture, the food, and the language. 
I felt that everything was so different from what I was used to, and I had a great time experiencing a new culture. 
I would definitely like to visit again in the future.
```

### Question2
```
I see. When visiting a new destination, do you prefer to travel alone or with companions? Why?
```

#### Ans2
```
I prefer to travel with companions 
because it is much safer than traveling alone. 
Going abroad can be quite risky, especially when you are in a place you do not know well. 
With companions, we can help each other when we feel worried or confused, and it makes the trip more comfortable and secure.
```

### Question3
```
Good points. 
Airports often introduce rules about baggage limits that might make travel less comfortable 
but help speed up security checks. 
Do you think such policies are necessary for travel? Why or why not?
```

#### Ans3
```
I think these rules are very important for travel 
because safety is the most important thing. 
I appreciate the security checks because they help ensure passenger safety. 
Of course, the extra checking time can make travel a little less convenient, 
but it is worth it because it reduces the risk of danger during the flight.
```

### Question4
```
Interesting. 
Do you think that traveling to new places will become more common or less common in the next ten years? Explain your thoughts.
```

#### Ans4
```
I think traveling to new places will become more common in the next ten years 
because transportation will become more convenient and efficient for people. 
For example, high-speed trains and other vehicles will be faster and more accessible than they are now. 
As a result, travel will become easier and more affordable, so more people will have the opportunity to visit new places.
```
